## Notes

- Toolchain: Node 20.9.0 in CI (`.circleci/config.yml` image `cimg/node:20.9.0`), pnpm 10.11.0 (`packageManager` in `package.json`). Jest (`jest-expo` preset) runs with `--maxWorkers=2` in CI.
- Tests live in a `specs/` folder next to the code: e.g. `src/utils/rtlUtils.ts` → `src/utils/specs/rtlUtils.spec.ts`. `ios/` and `android/` are generated by `expo prebuild` and git-ignored.
- The `.circleci/config.yml` job (`pnpm exec jest`) comes from upstream; on the fork only the `registry-gate` check reports, so nothing runs the full suite in CI here.
