FoxDesk Ai is looking forward to working with security researchers worldwide to keep FoxDesk Ai and our users safe. If you have found an issue in our systems/applications, please reach out to us.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please disclose it responsibly by contacting security@foxdeskai.com. This will enable us to review the vulnerability, fix it promptly, and reward you for your efforts.

Please try your best to describe a clear and realistic impact for your report, and please don't open any public issues on GitHub or social media; we're doing our best to respond as quickly as possible.

> Note: Please do not perform testing against FoxDesk Ai production services. Use a `self-hosted instance` to perform tests.

## Vulnerabilities we care about 🫣

- Remote command execution
- SQL Injection
- Authentication bypass
- Privilege escalation
- Cross-site scripting (XSS)
- Data exposure

## Non-Qualifying Vulnerabilities

- Reports from automated tools or scans
- Denial of service attacks
- Issues without a clear security impact
- Social engineering of staff or users

If you are unsure about the scope, please reach out to security@foxdeskai.com.

## Thanks

Thank you for keeping FoxDesk Ai and our users safe. 🙇
